📖OFFICIAL RULE BOOK

Objective

Be the first player to play all cards from your hand before your opponents!

Gameplay Basics

  • On your turn, match the top discard card by Color, Number, or Symbol.
  • If you have no playable card, tap the Draw Deck to draw 1 card.

Special Action Cards

  • Skip (⊘) Next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse (⇄) Reverses turn direction. In a 2-player match, acts as a Skip.
  • Draw Two (+2) Next player draws 2 cards and forfeits their turn (or stacks if matching).
  • Wild Can be played on any card; player chooses the next active color.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4) Player chooses active color, and next player draws 4 cards.

Calling UNO

When you have only 1 card remaining, you must press the flashing UNO! button before your turn ends!

The Flip Mechanic

Cards have two sides: a Light Side (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow) and a Dark Side (Pink, Teal, Purple, Orange). When a FLIP (🗘) card is played, the entire match flips over: your hand, opponent hands, draw pile, and discard stack!

Light Side Actions

  • Draw One (+1) Next player draws 1 card.
  • Skip (⊘) Skips the next player.
  • Reverse (⇄) Reverses direction of play.
  • Wild Changes color to any Light color.

Dark Side Actions (Brutal & Penalizing)

  • Draw Five (+5) Next player must draw 5 cards immediately!
  • Skip Everyone Skips every other player at the table—you immediately take another turn!
  • Dark Reverse Reverses direction under dark rules.
  • Wild Draw Color Player chooses a color; the victim must keep drawing from the deck until that specific color appears!

Extreme Rules & The Mercy Knockout

Show 'Em No Mercy is the toughest, fastest edition of UNO with cutthroat stacking rules.

Mercy Rule: If any player ever accumulates 25 or more cards in their hand at once, they are automatically knocked out of the match!

Attack Cards & Stacking

  • +4, +6, +10 Heavy penalties! All draw cards can be stacked on each other if you play an equal or higher draw card to pass the attack streak down the line.
  • Discard All Discards all cards in your hand matching the active color in a single turn.
  • Wild Reverse +4 (⇄+4) Reverses turn direction AND attacks the player behind you with 4 cards.
  • Skip All Gives you another immediate turn while skipping the rest of the table.

Every Card Is Wild

There are 112 cards and every card is wild. You never match colors or numbers, so any card may be played.

Wild Cards

  • Wild No action. You may call out a color, but it does not change anything in All Wild.
  • Wild Reverse Reverses the direction of play.
  • Wild Skip Skips the next player.
  • Wild Skip Two Skips the next two players.
  • Wild Draw Two Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
  • Wild Draw Four Next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.
  • Targeted Draw Two Choose another player to draw 2 cards; they keep their next turn.
  • Forced Swap You must choose another player and exchange entire hands.

Drawing

Because every card is playable, drawing is a strategic choice. You may draw 1 card instead of playing. After drawing, you may play only the card you just drew, or pass without playing it.

Starting & Winning

Deal 7 cards to each player. If the first discard is an action card, its action is ignored. When a player plays their final card, they win (with the Forced Swap action still resolving when applicable).
